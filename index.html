Current focus

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD

I study which stochastic information is required to predict the stability of finite-step stochastic gradient descent, especially near Edge-of-Stability regimes where continuous-time closures can miss discrete-step effects.

A controlled finite-sum construction shows that two SGD systems can share the same population Hessian and the same zero-order gradient-noise covariance at a reference point while having different exact finite-step second-moment stability thresholds.

q = (1 − ηλ)² + η²GΣ
ηc = 2λ / (λ² + GΣ)

Here GΣ captures state-dependent gradient-noise geometry. The claim is an explicit descriptor-insufficiency and stability result — not merely the observation that multiplicative noise exists.

Mathematical foundations

Infinite-Dimensional Dynamics & Mathematical Physics

My earlier and continuing mathematical work concerns invariant measures, Hamiltonian dynamics, singular solutions and Koopman/operator representations in infinite-dimensional spaces.

  • Invariant measures.
    Measures on Hilbert spaces invariant under Hamiltonian flows.
  • Singular dynamics.
    Continuation of differential-equation trajectories through singularity formation.
  • Operator methods.
    Koopman representations of infinite-dimensional Hamiltonian flows.
  • Functional analysis.
    Banach limits and finitely additive measures on L∞(R).
