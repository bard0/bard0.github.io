Completed project · v1.0

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD

Question: which stochastic information is sufficient to predict a finite-step stability or dynamical quantity?

An exact finite-sum construction shows that matching lower-order local descriptors need not fix the second-moment stability threshold when the noise geometry depends on state. Controlled matched-system tests support the added predictive value of this geometry, while a preregistered higher-order neural test remained negative.

Completed research case study

Spectral Reliability for Nonstationary Time Series

Question: when can a slow spectral object estimated from finite data be trusted, and when does it cease to be well defined?

Cross-scale invariant-subspace disagreement predicted estimation error in controlled regular-gap systems, while non-normal tests exposed a distinct admissibility failure. A projected boundary-complexification law closely matched fresh simulations; causal diagnostics predicted reliability better than they identified whether the source was estimation noise or smooth drift.
