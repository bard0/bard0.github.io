Current research

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD

My current project asks what information about stochastic gradients is actually needed to predict finite-step stability. I use exact discrete dynamics and mean-square/Lyapunov analysis rather than relying only on continuous-time diffusion approximations.

I constructed explicit finite-sum examples in which two SGD systems have the same population curvature and the same local gradient-noise covariance at a reference point, but different exact stability thresholds because the noise geometry changes differently with the state.

q=(1−ηλ)²+η²GΣ,   ηc=2λ/(λ²+GΣ)

Controlled matched-system experiments support the same picture numerically for both stability-boundary and sharpness-gap differences.

Mathematical research
