Recent and current research

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD — completed v1.0

Finite-step stability analysis of SGD using exact discrete stochastic dynamics and mean-square/Lyapunov methods. Constructed explicit finite-sum examples where equal population curvature and local gradient-noise covariance do not imply equal stability thresholds.

q=(1−ηλ)²+η²GΣ,   ηc=2λ/(λ²+GΣ)

Controlled matched-system comparisons support additional predictive value from state-dependent gradient-noise geometry for stability-boundary and sharpness-gap differences. The project also retained confirmatory negative evidence: a preregistered higher-order neural descriptor did not satisfy the frozen cross-regime robustness criteria.

Causal Spectral Reliability in Nonstationary Time Series — completed research case study

Studied local Koopman/EDMD spectra under finite data and nonstationarity, with emphasis on slow invariant subspaces, non-normality, spectral admissibility, and causal reliability diagnostics. Cross-scale subspace disagreement predicted estimation error in controlled regular-gap systems (AUROC up to about 0.83 for the cluster benchmark) but did not identify true drift. A projected boundary-complexification probability law matched fresh simulations with MAE about 0.00225 over 252 conditions. Near the spectral boundary, center uncertainty made naive point-risk estimates nonregular, while interval-valued risk remained calibrated in the controlled local family. A final matched-overlap benchmark found no reliable causal separation of estimation instability from smooth true drift.
