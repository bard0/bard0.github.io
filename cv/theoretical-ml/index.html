Research profile

Applied mathematician working on finite-step SGD and on spectral reliability for nonstationary time series. The common theme is stability: when a discrete stochastic optimizer becomes unstable, and when a local spectral estimate should or should not be trusted. Background in functional analysis, measure theory, operator methods, and dynamical systems.

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD

  • Exact finite-step analysis of SGD stability without reducing the dynamics to a Brownian or Langevin approximation.
  • Explicit finite-sum examples where zero-order local descriptors agree but exact stability thresholds differ.
  • Solvable stability relation q=(1−ηλ)²+η²GΣ with ηc=2λ/(λ²+GΣ).
  • Controlled matched-system comparisons: approximately 85% of stability-threshold differences and 98% of matched sharpness-gap differences explained by the noise-geometry model.
  • Matched controls, held-out evaluation, bootstrap/permutation inference, negative controls, and resumable checkpoints.

Nonstationary Time Series & Spectral Reliability

  • Online Koopman/EDMD estimation using only past observations.
  • Separate finite-sample spectral instability from genuine dynamical drift rather than treating every spectral change as a regime change.
  • Compare slow invariant subspaces across history lengths and time, with spectral-gap and non-normality checks.
  • Controlled operator tests show that global eigenvector conditioning does not by itself determine selected-subspace failure risk when the same non-normality is placed differently relative to the spectral boundary.
  • Current work is aimed at boundary-specific reliability diagnostics rather than a generic adaptive-window rule.
