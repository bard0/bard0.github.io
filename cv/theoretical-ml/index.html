Research profile

I am an applied mathematician working on the dynamics of stochastic optimization. My current focus is finite-step SGD near its stability boundary: how gradient-noise geometry changes the behavior of the discrete algorithm, and which local stochastic descriptors are actually sufficient to predict that behavior. My mathematical background is in functional analysis, measure theory, operator methods and dynamical systems.

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD

  • Built an exact finite-step framework for studying SGD stability without reducing the dynamics to a Brownian or Langevin approximation.
  • Constructed explicit finite-sum examples where standard zero-order local descriptors agree but the exact stability thresholds do not.
  • Derived the solvable stability relation q=(1−ηλ)²+η²GΣ with ηc=2λ/(λ²+GΣ).
  • In controlled matched-system comparisons, the noise-geometry model explained about 85% of stability-threshold differences and 98% of matched sharpness-gap differences.
  • Designed the computational work around falsification: matched controls, held-out evaluation, bootstrap/permutation inference, negative controls and resumable checkpoints.
