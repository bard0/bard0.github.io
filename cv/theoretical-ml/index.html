Research profile

Applied mathematician working on finite-step SGD, Edge of Stability, gradient-noise geometry, and stochastic stability. Background in functional analysis, measure theory, operator methods, and dynamical systems.

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD

  • Exact finite-step analysis of SGD stability without reducing the dynamics to a Brownian or Langevin approximation.
  • Explicit finite-sum examples where zero-order local descriptors agree but exact stability thresholds differ.
  • Solvable stability relation q=(1−ηλ)²+η²GΣ with ηc=2λ/(λ²+GΣ).
  • Controlled matched-system comparisons: approximately 85% of stability-threshold differences and 98% of matched sharpness-gap differences explained by the noise-geometry model.
  • Matched controls, held-out evaluation, bootstrap/permutation inference, negative controls, and resumable checkpoints.
