Research profile

Finite-Step Stochastic Dynamics of SGD

  • Developed an exact finite-step approach to SGD stability beyond purely Brownian/Langevin descriptions.
  • Constructed finite-sum counterexamples showing insufficiency of zero-order local stochastic descriptors for finite-step stability.
  • Derived an exact solvable stability condition q=(1−ηλ)²+η²GΣ and ηc=2λ/(λ²+GΣ).
  • In controlled matched-system comparisons, the noise-geometry model explained approximately 85% of stability-threshold differences and 98% of matched sharpness-gap differences.
  • Use falsification-first experimental design: matched controls, held-out evaluation, bootstrap/permutation inference, negative controls and reproducible checkpoints.
