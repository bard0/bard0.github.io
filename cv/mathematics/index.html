Academic profile

My mathematical work is centered on functional analysis, measure theory, operator methods, differential equations and infinite-dimensional dynamics. More recently, I have been bringing the same dynamical-systems viewpoint into optimization theory, especially finite-step SGD and stochastic stability.

Research areas

  • Invariant measures on infinite-dimensional spaces and Hamiltonian dynamics.
  • Koopman representations and operator methods for infinite-dimensional flows.
  • Continuation of differential-equation solutions through singularity formation.
  • Finitely additive measures, Banach limits and functional analysis.
  • Stochastic optimization, finite-step stability and gradient-noise geometry.
