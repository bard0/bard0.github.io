# Design and content notes

## Positioning

Primary external identity:

**Applied Mathematician · Machine Learning Researcher**

The site should communicate three things within the first screen:

1. strong mathematical background;
2. an active, original theoretical-ML research program;
3. reproducible computational work rather than purely abstract positioning.

## Visual system

- Layout: editorial/academic, not a startup landing page.
- Accent: restrained research blue (`#285ea8`).
- Typography: system sans for UI/body + Georgia serif for research titles.
- Motion: intentionally minimal.
- Theme: automatic system preference plus manual dark/light toggle.
- Maximum content width: 1120 px.

## Content hierarchy

1. Hero / identity
2. Current research contribution
3. Mathematical foundations
4. Publications
5. Talks
6. Education + research experience
7. CV downloads
8. Contact

## What not to put on the homepage

- internal experiment identifiers (`M9`, `G8.10`, etc.);
- a chronological log of failed hypotheses;
- unverified novelty ratings;
- claims that a neural result generalized if it did not pass the frozen cross-regime criterion;
- long tool lists that crowd out the scientific contribution.

Negative results remain valuable in the repository and paper; the homepage should summarize the resulting research boundary rather than the internal sequence of attempts.

## Future upgrades

Priority order:

1. higher-resolution portrait;
2. public preprint/manuscript card when available;
3. one publication-quality figure from the SGD work;
4. short Research Notes section for expository writing;
5. custom domain;
6. optional privacy-friendly analytics only if there is a concrete use case.
